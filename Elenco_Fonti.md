# English Version

This document provides a comprehensive bibliography of all the raw text sources used to procedurally generate this Italian Spellchecker Dictionary base. 

To ensure the final dictionary could be legally released under the highly permissive **MIT License** (avoiding any GPL or viral copyleft restrictions), we could not simply copy existing copyrighted dictionaries. Instead, we built a custom python algorithm that read hundreds of selected **Public Domain (CC0)** and **MIT-licensed** texts, extracting the most common and statistically relevant words.

The sources listed above cover various domains of the Italian language to guarantee a complete and modern vocabulary:
1. **Basic Lexicon:** A foundational MIT-licensed list of common Italian words.
2. **Law & Bureaucracy:** The Italian Constitution, EU Directives (EUR-Lex), and standard privacy/legal templates to capture modern legal terminology.
3. **Science & Economics:** Annual reports from the Italian National Institute of Statistics (ISTAT) and the Bank of Italy.
4. **Technology:** Open-source software documentation (Godot, Vue.js) to capture modern IT jargon.
5. **Taxonomy:** CC0 database dumps from Wikidata to catalog animals, plants, food, and daily objects.
6. **Classic Literature:** Over 70 timeless Italian novels and essays from Project Gutenberg (Pirandello, Verga, Svevo, Salgari) to capture the richness of narrative language.
